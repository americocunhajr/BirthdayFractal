BEHIND THE IMAGES

Beautiful pictures.
Precise mathematics.

The companion paper connects classical complex dynamics to three concrete results for date polynomials.

PROPOSITION 01

An isolated negative root

For dates in years 1000–2999 with day 15–19 or 27–29, exactly one root lies outside the disk |z| < 5/2. It is real, negative, and simple.

Rouché’s theorem
PROPOSITION 02

Dynamics on the positive axis

For polynomials with nonnegative coefficients, a root at zero attracts every positive real starting point. When p(0) > 0, every such orbit eventually leaves the positive half-line.

Global convergence
PROPOSITION 03 · COROLLARY 01

When Newton does not converge

Mandelbrot’s birthday polynomial exchanges two real intervals without approaching a root. Within these intervals lies a repelling period-two orbit.

K₋ = [−0.7626, −0.6240]
K₊ = [0.1580, 0.2012]

COMPUTATION & PROOF

Two complementary approaches

The interactive studio provides approximate, double-precision pictures computed on your device. The mathematical results of the paper rely on analytical arguments and separate exact-arithmetic certificates.

The complete scientific manuscript, data, and certified computations are maintained as part of the research package; the live generator is an educational companion, not a substitute for those verifications.
