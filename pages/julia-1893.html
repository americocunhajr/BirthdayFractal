02 / COMPLEX DYNAMICS

What Newton’s method reveals

Np(z) = z − p(z)/p′(z)

Newton's method preserves the real axis wherever the map is defined. Consequently, a real starting point cannot converge to one of the nonreal roots. Basins of conjugate roots occur in reflected pairs across the real axis.

03 / WHY THIS DATE MATTERS

A mathematical insight

The absence of real roots alone does not prove that the entire real axis is in the Julia set. Such a conclusion also requires excluding non-root stable components.
