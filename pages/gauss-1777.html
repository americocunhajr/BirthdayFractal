01 / FROM DATE TO POLYNOMIAL

A polynomial from eight digits

The date 30/04/1777 is read as the coefficients of a polynomial, from highest degree to constant term. Any initial zero coefficient is discarded.

p(z) = 3z⁷ + 4z⁴ + z³ + 7z² + 7z + 7

02 / COMPLEX DYNAMICS

What Newton’s method reveals

Np(z) = z − p(z)/p′(z)

Magnifications near poles of the Newton map show multiple attraction basins meeting at fine scales. The basin boundaries form the Julia set, and inverse images of Julia-set points also lie in the Julia set.

03 / WHY THIS DATE MATTERS

A mathematical insight

This date illustrates the Wada phenomenon: distinct root basins have a common boundary when considered as entire basins, even when individual colored regions are disconnected.
