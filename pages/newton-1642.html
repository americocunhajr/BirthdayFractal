NEWTON FRACTALS · MATHEMATICAL HISTORY

Isaac Newton’s birthday
Newton fractal.

25 December 1642 · 25/12/1642

Isaac Newton was born on 25 December 1642 in the Julian calendar, then in use in England. The corresponding Gregorian date is 4 January 1643. These different dates encode different polynomials, so the two Newton fractals are not the same dynamical picture.

01 / FROM DATE TO POLYNOMIAL

A polynomial from eight digits

The date 25/12/1642 is read as the coefficients of a polynomial, from highest degree to constant term. Any initial zero coefficient is discarded.

p(z) = 2z⁷ + 5z⁶ + z⁵ + 2z⁴ + z³ + 6z² + 4z + 2

02 / COMPLEX DYNAMICS

What Newton’s method reveals

Np(z) = z − p(z)/p′(z)

For the Julian date, the polynomial has degree seven. For the Gregorian date 04/01/1643, the leading zero reduces the degree to six. The geometry of each Newton fractal shows basins of attraction for the corresponding polynomial roots, with Julia-set boundaries between the basins.

03 / WHY THIS DATE MATTERS

A mathematical insight

The calendar convention changes the mathematical system itself. This example illustrates how an apparently minor choice in representing a date changes the resulting polynomial and Newton map.
