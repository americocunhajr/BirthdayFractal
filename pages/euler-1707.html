Leonhard Euler’s birthday
Newton fractal.

15 April 1707 · 15/04/1707

Euler's date falls within the day range 15–19. Proposition 1 in the accompanying manuscript establishes that every such date in years 1000–2999 has exactly one simple negative real root outside the disk |z| < 5/2; the other six roots lie inside.

01 / FROM DATE TO POLYNOMIAL

A polynomial from eight digits

The date 15/04/1707 is read as the coefficients of a polynomial, from highest degree to constant term. Any initial zero coefficient is discarded.

p(z) = z⁷ + 5z⁶ + 4z⁴ + z³ + 7z² + 7

02 / COMPLEX DYNAMICS

What Newton’s method reveals

Np(z) = z − p(z)/p′(z)

The isolated root of Euler's polynomial is approximately −5.15. The corresponding basin may lie partly outside a visualization window focused on the other roots; the isolated root is nevertheless included in the Newton map.

03 / WHY THIS DATE MATTERS

A mathematical insight

The root separation is certified by Rouché's theorem, independently of any plotted pixels. It is an exact mathematical consequence of constraints on calendar-date digits.
